# Official evaluation notes

`official-evaluation-history.csv` transcribes the ten completed-evaluation emails
retained in the registered Team Smoop mailbox. Every email reports six feasible
outputs, so the observed campaign record is **60/60 feasible hidden-instance
executions**.

The numbers `1` through `10` are **chronological labels used by this retrospective**.
They are not **organizer-assigned submission identifiers**.

The `descriptive_raw_sum` column adds the six hidden-instance objectives. It is
useful for tracking broad movement over time, but it is **not the competition
score**. OGC 2026 ranked teams independently by instance; a lower pooled sum can
coexist with a weaker competitive outcome when gains and regressions occur on
different instances.
