# Official result notes

`official-submissions.csv` transcribes the ten organizer evaluation vectors preserved
in the private campaign record. Every row contains six feasible outputs, so the
campaign record is 60/60 feasible hidden-instance executions.

The `raw_sum` column is a descriptive sum across the six hidden instances. It is
useful for tracking broad movement over time, but it is **not the competition
score**. OGC 2026 used ordinal, instance-specific ranking; a lower aggregate sum
can coexist with a weaker competitive rank if improvements and regressions fall on
different instances.

Submission 10 / Candidate 23 is the terminal submitted solver. The exact final
preliminary-round rank was not preserved in the available official records.
