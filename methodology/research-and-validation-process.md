# Research and validation process

The campaign separated record types that are often conflated in heuristic
optimization work:

1. **Question:** what decision or bottleneck might change?
2. **Opportunity evidence:** does the required event or action surface actually
   occur under the accepted solver?
3. **Mechanism:** what bounded treatment could exploit that opportunity?
4. **Causal experiment:** does the frozen treatment beat matched controls while
   remaining checker-valid and inside the wall-time envelope?
5. **Promotion decision:** should the mechanism enter the solver, remain deferred,
   or close as tested?

## Controls used

- Source identities and release packages were frozen by SHA-256.
- Fixed-wall comparisons used paired, reversed-order, repeated same-binary, or
  explicit noise controls where attribution mattered.
- Engagement telemetry was not treated as objective improvement.
- Stored artifacts were reloaded and replayed from disk; in-memory success was
  insufficient.
- Every candidate presented to the parent was passed through the organizer checker
  before selection.
- Negative results closed the exact tested lane instead of silently changing the
  panel, threshold, trigger, or mechanism.
- A protected feasible path and emergency fallback remained available while
  experimental workers searched.

## Structured research campaign

The formal research program recorded **49 separately reviewed investigations**.
One investigation bound one research question to one specialist response, a
provenance record, and a separate review record. The accepted reports produced:

- 27 recommendations for a bounded test;
- 13 recommendations to close the proposed direction;
- 9 deferrals pending a prerequisite.

Six selected opportunity-first empirical gates reached valid terminal kills. Their
specific mechanisms differed, but the shared result was the same: the current
solver exposed too little of the required opportunity to justify a more expensive
treatment. The public report presents that decision rule rather than requiring the
reader to learn the private role, batch, and campaign identifiers.

The formal workflow ended before its originally planned 80 roles. The terminal
candidate sprint used a lighter direct-research and implementation loop. This is
not hidden as process failure or recast as completion: high-control review was
useful for pruning and provenance, but its coordination latency exceeded its value
under the final competition deadline.

## Evidence hierarchy

Public claims follow this order:

1. direct organizer evaluation and credential records;
2. exact private artifacts and checker-backed local results;
3. reproducible arithmetic derived from those records;
4. interpretation, explicitly bounded by the underlying evidence.

Public v1.1 applies this rule to the result history itself. All ten rows were
reconciled against the direct completed-evaluation emails after v1.0 was found to
contain unsupported intermediate values.

No exact rank, award, finalist, or optimality claim is made.
